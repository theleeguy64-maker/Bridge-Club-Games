## CRITICAL caveat — what "Open" actually means at this club

The schedule labels cells "Open", but **ACBL recent-results history shows every single Open game in the last 100 sessions has had an MP cap baked in**:

| Title as it actually ran | Cap | Sessions in last 100 | Avg tables | Avg pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Open <=4 MP` | ≤4 MP | 2 | 3.5 | **~7** |
| `Open <=8 MP` | ≤8 MP | 4 | 4.5 | **~9** |
| `Open 8 MP` | 8 MP | 1 | 3 | **~6** |

These are **beginner games**, 6–9 pairs each.

The real-field games on this club are MP-capped at 3000 (you're 4000 → excluded):

| Event | Sessions/week | Avg tables | Avg pairs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `3000 S1` (morning) | 7 | 24 | **48** |
| `3000 S3` (afternoon) | 7 | 18 | **36** |
| `799 Vero S1` (morning) | 5 | 22 | **44** |

**Net result:** as a 4000 MP player, there is essentially **no SE Carolina game you're eligible for with a real field**.

The only exception is during **NAP (North American Pairs) qualifier weeks** — twice a year — when the "Open NAP" tier is offered with no MP ceiling.
